---
title: "Website vs Google Ads: what should come first?"
description: "A clear comparison for local businesses deciding whether to improve their website or start Google Ads."
category: "Google Ads"
---

Google Ads can bring targeted traffic faster, but ads work best when they send people to a page that is ready for them. For many local businesses, the website should come first.

## Start with the website when

- Your current site looks outdated
- Your phone number and forms are hard to find
- Your services are not explained clearly
- Your site is not mobile-friendly
- You do not have a strong page to send ad traffic to

## Consider ads when

- Your website or landing page is ready
- You can handle more calls or form leads
- Your jobs have enough value to support ad spend
- You can test for at least a few months

## The practical order

Build the foundation first, then add traffic. Ads cannot fix a confusing website, and a good website can support both organic local visibility and paid campaigns.
