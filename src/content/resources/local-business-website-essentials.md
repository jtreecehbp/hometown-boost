title: "What should a local business website include?"
description: "A plain-English checklist for local business owners who want a website that builds trust and makes contact easy."

---

A local business website does not need to be complicated. It needs to answer the questions real customers ask before they call, visit, or request a quote.

## The basics

- Clear business name and service type
- Phone number and contact form
- Service area or location
- Hours when relevant
- Main services explained in plain language
- Mobile-friendly layout
- Trust signals such as licenses, photos, reviews, or years of experience if they are real and provided by the business

## What helps customers act

Customers should not have to hunt for the next step. Put call buttons, request-a-quote buttons, and contact forms in obvious places. Make sure the site works well on a phone, because many local searches happen there.

## What helps search engines understand the business

Each page should have a clear title, useful headings, local service terms, and accurate business information. This is not a ranking guarantee. It is the foundation that helps search engines and customers understand what the business does.
