description: "A simple look at why a complete Google Business Profile helps local customers find, trust, and contact your business."
category: "Google Business Profile"
---

For many local customers, your Google Business Profile is the first place they see your business. It can show your services, hours, reviews, photos, location details, and ways to contact you.

## What to keep accurate

- Business name
- Phone number
- Website link
- Hours
- Service categories
- Service area
- Photos
- Services

## How it works with your website

Your website and Google profile should support each other. The profile helps customers find you in local search and maps. The website gives them more detail, builds trust, and gives them a place to take the next step.

## Keep expectations honest

Optimizing a profile does not guarantee rankings or leads. It does help create a cleaner, more complete local presence.
